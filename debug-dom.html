Frequently Asked Questions

Still have questions? Visit our full FAQ→
What metadata is removed?+

We remove EXIF data (GPS coordinates, camera make/model, serial number, timestamps), IPTC data (captions, keywords), and XMP data. The image pixels remain untouched.

Does this reduce image quality?+

No. Metadata is separate from the image data. Removing it does not re-compress or alter the pixels in any way.

Is anything uploaded to a server?+

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your photos never leave your device â€” you can verify this in your browser's network tab.

What about social networks?+

Some social networks strip metadata on upload, but not all. Stripping at the source means you're not relying on any platform's policy.
